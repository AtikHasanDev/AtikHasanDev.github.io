Assignment 9 · Repair booking platform

FixMate

A device and home appliance repair booking application. Customers request a repair, follow its progress, and pay after completion. Technicians and administrators have dedicated dashboards.

Visit live projectView client on GitHub

Technology stack

  • Next.js
  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Tailwind CSS
  • DaisyUI
  • Express.js
  • MongoDB
  • Better Auth
  • Stripe

What it does

  • Search and filter approved repair services by category and service mode, with sorting and pagination.
  • Register as a customer or technician and use dashboards with role specific permissions.
  • Request repairs with optional damage photos, track job progress, and cancel pending requests.
  • Technicians manage services, update repair status, and set final pricing. Administrators moderate services and review revenue.
  • Pay for completed repairs with Stripe test Checkout and view payment history.

Development challenges

Technical challenges reflected in the project's features and implementation:

  • Enforcing roles and ownership consistently across a Next.js frontend and an Express API.
  • Keeping booking progress, cancellations, final pricing, and payment eligibility consistent.
  • Verifying signed Stripe webhooks and preventing duplicate payment fulfillment.

Potential improvements

Suggested next steps for a future version:

  • Expand automated browser coverage for the full repair lifecycle.
  • Add appointment scheduling and clearer technician availability windows.
